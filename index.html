Our Approach

From site survey to live control
in four steps

01.

Assess

We audit every turbine, inverter and comms link on site — OEM models, capacities and protocols — before a single tag is mapped.

02.

Build

Rook RTUs and the SCADA stack are configured for your exact OEM mix and factory-tested in house, so what ships is already proven.

03.

Deploy

Our engineers install over GSM or OFC, validate every signal against the plant, and hand over a system signed off for CEA and SLDC compliance.

04.

Operate

Live dashboards, alarms and custom reports run at head office and site office, with remote support long after handover.
